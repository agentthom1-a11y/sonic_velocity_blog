## Content Guidelines
1. **Executive Summary**: Always start with a summary.
2. **Headings**: Use `##` and `###`. Name them like "SIGNAL ANALYSIS", "ARTIFACT 01", "CULTURAL VELOCITY".
3. **Blockquotes**: Use `> ` for critical "Overheard" or "Signal Intercept" quotes.
4. **Vocabulary**: Use words like *velocity, transmission, artifact, signal, noise, neural, copy-culture, koplo-logic, hybridity*.
5. **Perspective**: Focus on how AI and technology are being *misused* or *remixed* by real people in Southeast Asia (specifically Indonesia).
